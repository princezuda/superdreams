Emberwake — credits & asset record

All visuals are generated at runtime in code: pixel-art characters, monsters and portraits (js/pixel.js),
the tile atlas (js/atlas.js), terrain/buildings/props (js/world.js). All sound effects are Web Audio synthesis
(js/audio.js on the shared kit). Story, characters, names and world are original.

Third-party code: three.js r187 (MIT), vendored by the site.

External assets used: none.
Rejected assets: none considered — the pixel-diorama look is easier to keep coherent when every texel is painted by the same code.
