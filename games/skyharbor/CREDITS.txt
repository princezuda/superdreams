Skyharbor — asset credits

All geometry, textures, shaders and sounds in Skyharbor are procedural and original to this game:
- Terrain, planets, skies, water, buildings and the twelve landmarks are generated in code (three.js r187 + TSL).
- Sound effects come from the shared Superdreams kit synth (Web Audio), no audio files.
- Fonts: system UI fonts only.

External assets used: none.
Rejected assets: none considered — the procedural approach covered every need.
