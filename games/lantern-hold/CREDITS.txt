Lantern Hold — credits

All art is procedural and original: low-poly models kitbashed from three.js primitives (models.js),
terrain/road/ice/lava/rift shaders written in TSL (world.js), UI icons drawn as inline SVG in code (icons.js),
and sound effects synthesised at runtime with the Super Dreams kit (Web Audio).

Third-party assets used: none.
Assets considered and rejected: none downloaded (network-restricted build; procedural art was the plan from the start).

Engine: three.js r187 (MIT), vendored at /vendor/three/.
