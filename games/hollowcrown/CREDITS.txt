The Hollow Crown — credits

All art in this game is procedural: geometry built from three.js primitives, textures and item icons painted at
runtime with Canvas 2D, skies/water/paper grain generated in TSL shaders. No third-party art, audio or fonts are used.
Sound effects are synthesised at runtime by the shared Superdreams kit.

Story, characters, dialog and world (Wrenfold, Tamsin, Queen Elowen, Sir Brannoch, Mabel Crust, Miller Hobb, Fennick,
Granny Thistle, Grumbleroot, Keeper Marlow, Corvin the Magpie King) are original to this game.
Inspired by the structure of classic 90s painted adventure games; no names, characters, maps or UI from any of them.

Rejected / not used:
- No downloaded assets were evaluated; the painterly look is achieved procedurally so the remix (season, banner colour)
  can repaint every scene live.

Engine: three.js r187 (MIT), vendored at /vendor/three.
