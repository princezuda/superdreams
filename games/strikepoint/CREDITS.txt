Strikepoint — asset credits
===========================

All 3D geometry (weapons, soldiers, drones, the Overseer gunship, buildings, props, vegetation) is procedural,
built from primitives in code. All sound effects are synthesised at runtime with the Web Audio API. No music.

Textures (CC0, Poly Haven — https://polyhaven.com/license), downloaded at 1K and recompressed:
colour 1024px JPG q72, normal 512px JPG q82, roughness 256px greyscale JPG q80. Subjects verified visually
(contact sheet) before use.
- Asphalt 02 (asphalt_02) by Rob Tuytel — Dockyard / plant / island roads
- Concrete Wall 008 (concrete_wall_008) by Dario Barresi, Charlotte Baglioni — Concrete walls, barriers, slabs
- Corrugated Iron 02 (corrugated_iron_02) by Jenelle van Heerden, Sergej Majboroda — Shipping containers, sheds, warehouses
- Plastered Wall (plastered_wall) by Amal Kumar — Village houses
- Cobblestone Floor 08 (cobblestone_floor_08) by Rob Tuytel — Village streets
- Brown Planks 05 (brown_planks_05) by Rob Tuytel — Crates, pallets, wooden structures
- Rusty Metal 02 (rusty_metal_02) by Rob Tuytel — Ship hull, pipe racks, scrap
- Metal Plate (metal_plate) by Rob Tuytel — Catwalks, stairs, machinery
- Clay Roof Tiles (clay_roof_tiles) by Amal Kumar — Village roofs
- Sparse Grass (sparse_grass) by Amal Kumar — Village hills, island terrain
- Brick Wall 09 (brick_wall_09) by Rob Tuytel, Matterfield — Pump house, turbine hall, field walls

Rejected / not used:
- ambientCG texture sets (Concrete034 etc.): CC0 and preferred, but the download host (acg-download.struffelproductions.com)
  is blocked by the sandbox network proxy, so they could not be fetched. Poly Haven equivalents were used instead.
- Poly Haven 'rusty_metal_02' was kept but tinted down in code: the raw colour map is very bright/orange for machinery.
- No CC-BY / CC-BY-NC / No-Derivatives assets are used.
