Apex Drift — asset credits
==========================

All 3D geometry (cars, terrain, roads, barriers, gantry, trees, buildings, props), the TSL sky/water/snow shaders,
canvas-painted textures (banner, chevrons, wave normals, smoke) and all Web Audio sound effects are generated in
code in this folder plus the shared kit. No music (deferred to the Fable pass). Track names, driver names and the
car design are original.

Textures (CC0, Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/license), downloaded at 1K and recompressed:
colour 1024px (snow 512px) JPG q74, normal 512px JPG q82. Subjects checked on a contact sheet before use.
- Asphalt Track (asphalt_track) by Dimitrios Savva: road surface on all tracks, city ground streets
- Aerial Grass Rock (aerial_grass_rock) by Rob Tuytel: Amber Coast hillsides
- Rock Face 03 (rock_face_03) by Dario Barresi (photography) and Rico Cilliers (processing): cliffs, rocks,
  coast gravel run-off and beach sand (tinted), alpine rock faces (triplanar)
- Snow 02 (snow_02) by Rob Tuytel: Frostpeak Pass snow detail and normal (colour used at low weight, because
  the raw map has dark twig marks)

Rejected / not used:
- snow_field_aerial: the 1K diffuse download returned an error document, not an image. Dropped.
- asphalt_track roughness map: almost uniform at 1K (456 bytes after compression), so a constant roughness is
  used instead.
- ambientCG sets: CC0 and preferred, but the download host is blocked by the sandbox network proxy (the same
  finding as Strikepoint).
- No CC-BY, CC-BY-NC or No-Derivatives assets are used.
